Fluffy Pancakes

Description

These pancakes are light and fluffy, perfect for a weekend breakfast or brunch. They are easy to make and can be served with your favorite toppings such as syrup, fresh

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons white sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 large egg, beaten

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
  3. In another bowl, mix milk, vegetable oil, and beaten egg.
  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
  5. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat.
  6. Pour ¼ cup of batter for each pancake.
  7. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook until golden brown.
  8. Serve hot with your favorite toppings.
